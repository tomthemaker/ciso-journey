Fedlex's English is a convenience translation without legal force: see the [Federal Chancellery](https://www.bk.admin.ch/en/english). The Chancellery states German, French and Italian versions of federal laws are equally legally binding ([languages](https://www.bk.admin.ch/en/languages)); the counterparts above are locators, not verified full-text retrievals. English EU OJ texts are official-language versions. The EU locators above designate adopted original acts, not proposals and not a statement of their present amended form. EUR-Lex describes [consolidation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/EN/legal-content/glossary/consolidation.html) as a documentation tool without legal effect; the official acts and amendments must be checked separately. For example the indexed [GDPR consolidated PDF](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?qid=1789616612250&uri=CELEX%3A02016R0679-20160504) is dated 2016-05-04, not evidence of current law.

## Review and reuse follow-up

- A qualified human legal reviewer must obtain and inspect authoritative-language text, amendments, corrigenda, current versions and any relevant national implementation; distinguish enacted measures from proposals and verify all dates and claims at time of use. Direct access failures remain unresolved for this route; do not bypass challenges or substitute unofficial mirrors.
- O002 remains open: industry, employer scale, canton, EU exposure, entity roles, data flows and facts needed to assess applicability are unknown. No obligation, deadline or learner-specific conclusion follows from these locators.
- Before publication, check source reuse and third-party rights. [EUR-Lex's legal notice](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/content/legal-notice/legal-notice.html?locale=en) distinguishes legal documents from CC BY 4.0 editorial/consolidated material and notes exceptions; Swiss reuse terms and any third-party material need a separate check. This inventory links sources and reproduces no legal text.
- D008/D022 and the [content contract](CONTENT_CONTRACT.md) remain in force: retrieval, indexed excerpts and agent review do not constitute independent qualified legal or educator verification. Do not upgrade historical C01/C02 source or content status; any later source/content revision and review must follow that contract.
