# Pilot progress view

Refs [#18](https://github.com/tomthemaker/ciso-journey/issues/18). The initial increment reported pilot data; the 2026-10-29 extension below includes portfolio history. The validated R2 release gate remains open.

The Progress route derives a read-only report at render time from the validated local snapshot. The event window is the current local calendar day and preceding 29 local days, inclusive; UTC instants after the displayed as-of instant are excluded and counted. Each activity day shows the numerator and denominator for observed self-rated reviews and keyed MCQ attempts. Retry counts compare exact question revisions with earlier attempts, including earlier attempts outside the window. Historical missing content remains counted by recorded outcome and flagged. Constructed responses remain ungraded; self-assessments are grouped by exact rubric revision and criterion, with missing anchors counted separately. No scores are averaged across rubric criteria or versions.

Estimated recall is the arithmetic mean of per-card FSRS retrievability for active reviewed exact revisions, based on the latest eligible review state at the as-of instant. The existing deterministic adapter pins ts-fsrs 5.4.2 / FSRS-6.0 and its parameters; this estimate is neither observed recall nor competency. Suspended, archived and never-reviewed cards are excluded. Snapshot task, reading and legacy scores have no event timestamps; they cannot support historical trends. Resource counts use each tracked resource's last recorded progress at or before as-of; evidence counts are metadata presence, not quality. No learner content is copied into this document.

Validation: `npm test`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm run build`, and `npm run test:browser -- --workers=2` with Node 24.4.1. The browser suite includes Pages subpath and mobile flows. Final candidate and results are in the delivery PR. Independent review and the issue's remaining R2 dependencies are separate gates.

## Provisional curriculum and portfolio extension, 2026-10-29

Progress now reads the optional D038 portfolio namespace without writing it. A chosen 30/90/365-local-day count and an all-history count identify the appraisal sample; changing the window only rerenders the projection. Each eligible dated entry shows its calendar quarter, competency, reported level, exact scope, rationale, self-assessment provenance and evidence/response reference counts. Saved case responses show their IDs, submission instants and pinned scenario/rubric revisions; missing historical content is marked unavailable rather than replaced by a current revision. Exact competency, scope and provenance permit a prior reported-level comparison; other entries remain raw history. All six competencies show a no-data state. Future appraisal dates are excluded at the local as-of day and counted with other future events. Portfolio evidence has no creation date, so only its present snapshot count is shown. Case rubric criteria, legacy 0–4 ratings, observed recall, FSRS estimates, MCQ attempts, reading, tasks and resources remain separate. These are provisional learner self-reports and AI-draft practice signals, not independent assessment or readiness.

Unit coverage exercises a quarter boundary, exact-scope comparison, future exclusion, pinned unavailable content and projection immutability. Local browser coverage exercises evidence/appraisal entry, Progress display, reload and unchanged IndexedDB snapshot alongside the established mobile, keyboard, practice and Pages flows. Exact candidate and check results are in the delivery PR. No content or storage contract changes are introduced.
