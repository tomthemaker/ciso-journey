End goal

A portfolio containing an enterprise security architecture, NIST CSF assessment, risk register, ISMS outline, control assessment, incident tabletop, 3-year strategy and board report.

Legacy competency scores

Update ratings when you can demonstrate the skill. 0 = no experience, 1 = basic, 2 = working knowledge, 3 = advanced, 4 = can lead independently.

How to assess yourself

For every capability ask: Can I explain it? Apply it? Evaluate alternatives and tradeoffs? Govern it at enterprise level?

Target after 18 months: governance, risk and compliance at 3/4; architecture and operations at 2–3/4; executive reporting at 4/4. These targets are aspirational and require practical evidence.
